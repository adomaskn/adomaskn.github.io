Pre-Uni days

From my early days, the topics that I have been most interested in were Mathematics, Geography, Physics, and Chemistry. In school, I participated in many national competitions and olympiads to test my skills and I was proficient in these subjects. Given my current involvement in programming, it might be surprising to learn that I didn't study it at all during my school years. To be honest, I did learn some web development, but it wasn't my favorite. However, I had a keen interest in computers themselves and built my own computer from scratch when I was around 15, following a Linus Tech Tips tutorial. Back then, I was an avid gamer and wanted to upgrade from my PS3 to play more exciting games. In addition to all of the aforementioned, I was also in a choir from the 1st grade to the 12th and started playing football around the age of 9.

Uni days

I chose the topic that I am going to be studying on a whim. I couldn't decide between Physics and Mathematics, but the latter got my first pick and I got accepted to Vilnius University. My start at the uni (2019) wasn't the best and I even managed to fail a combinatorics class in my first semester. The global pandemic surprisingly helped me academically because I learned that studying is mainly done on your own. This changed my view because now I knew that the only person that can push you towards your goals is YOU. Uni also gave me a basic understanding of programming and its capabilities. I started learning Python and later deepened it through self-learning and the Mimo app.
